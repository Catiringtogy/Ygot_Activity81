Welcome to the TXT Universe!

TOMORROW X TOGETHER, also known as TXT, is a five-member South Korean boy group under BIGHIT MUSIC. They debuted on March 4, 2019, with their first mini-album, The Dream Chapter: STAR. Their name represents different individuals coming together through one shared dream to build a better tomorrow.


Through imaginative storytelling, emotional lyrics, and constantly evolving musical styles, TXT explores friendship, growing up, love, loneliness, temptation, and the challenges of finding one's place in the world. Each album adds another chapter to their musical journey.

DISCOGRAPHY

  • The Dream Chapter: STAR (2019)

    • Debut mini-album · March 4, 2019
    • The beginning of TXT's story, capturing the excitement of meeting someone special and discovering a new world together.
    • Featured song: “CROWN” (어느날 머리에서 뿔이 자랐다)

  • The Dream Chapter: MAGIC (2019)

    • First studio album · October 21, 2019
    • An energetic adventure about friendship, youth, and the magical moments that make growing up unforgettable.
    • Featured song: “9 and Three Quarters (Run Away)”

  • The Dream Chapter: ETERNITY (2020)

    • Second mini-album · May 18, 2020
    • A darker chapter exploring friendship, distance, and the fear of losing the people who matter most.
    • Featured song: “Can't You See Me?” (세계가 불타버린 밤, 우린...)

  • Minisode 1: Blue Hour (2020)

    • Third mini-album · October 26, 2020
    • A colorful yet nostalgic look at youth, connection, and the strange feeling of being together while the world changes around you.
    • Featured song: “Blue Hour” (5시 53분의 하늘에서 발견한 너와 나)

  • The Chaos Chapter: Freeze

    • Second studio album · May 31, 2021
    • A powerful portrayal of a world that feels frozen, until love arrives and changes everything.
    • Featured song: “0X1=LOVESONG (I Know I Love You)”

  • The Chaos Chapter: Fight or Escape (2021)

    • Repackage album · August 17, 2021
    • A continuation of the previous album's emotional world, focusing on choosing love and fighting against a painful reality.
    • Featured song: “LO$ER=LO♡ER”

  • Minisode 2: Thursday's Child (2022)

    • Fourth mini-album · May 9, 2022
    • An honest look at heartbreak, anger, sadness, and the difficult process of learning to live after a relationship ends.

  • The Name Chapter: Temptation (2023)

    • Fifth mini-album · January 27, 2023
    • A dreamy exploration of temptation, freedom, and the struggle between remaining in a comfortable world and facing reality.
